About

I am a third-year student in Computer Science and Telecommunications at EPAC (École Polytechnique d’Abomey-Calavi). The programme covers cybersecurity, software engineering and telecommunications. Alongside it, I study data science and machine learning independently, through the DataCamp Associate Data Scientist track and personal projects.

My main project looks at mobile network coverage in Benin, using cell-tower and population data to identify underserved areas. I am also working on a telecom customer churn model. Next, I plan to work on crop disease detection, flood and rainfall prediction, and text classification for Fon and Yoruba.
